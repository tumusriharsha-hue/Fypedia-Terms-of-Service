Fypedia Support

Help for the Fypedia learning app

Contact support

Email snaply.dev@gmail.com for account help, technical issues, feedback, or feature requests.

When contacting support, include a short description of the issue, the device and iOS version, and the app version if available. Never send your password, access token, or other private credentials.

Account and sign-in help

Make sure your device is connected to the internet. If you just created an account, complete email verification before signing in. If your session has expired, sign out and sign in again.

Feed and video issues

Fypedia depends on YouTube availability and network access. If a feed cannot be prepared, try again later or choose another topic. Some videos may become unavailable, private, age-restricted, or unavailable for embedding.

Questions and results

Questions are generated from available video information and may occasionally be unavailable because a transcript or generation service is temporarily unavailable. Try again later if question preparation fails.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account from Settings inside the Fypedia app. After successful deletion, the app clears local account data and signs you out. Contact support if deletion fails or you need help confirming the request.
